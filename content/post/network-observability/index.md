## The Limitations of Traditional Network Monitoring

Traditional network monitoring is focused on collecting data from network devices, such as routers and switches. This data can be used to track the health and performance of the network, but it does not provide a complete picture of what is happening on the network.

## What is Network Observability?

Network observability is a new approach to network monitoring that is designed to provide deeper insights into the health and performance of today's complex networks. Network observability goes beyond traditional network monitoring by collecting data from a variety of sources, including:

*   **Network devices:** Network observability collects data from network devices, such as routers and switches.
*   **Applications:** Network observability collects data from applications, such as web servers and databases.
*   **Users:** Network observability collects data from users, such as their location and device type.

## The Three Pillars of Observability

Network observability is based on the three pillars of observability:

*   **Metrics:** Metrics are numerical measurements of the health and performance of the network.
*   **Traces:** Traces are records of the path that a request takes through the network.
*   **Logs:** Logs are records of events that have occurred on the network.

## Benefits of Network Observability

Network observability can provide a number of benefits, including:

*   **Deeper insights:** Network observability can provide deeper insights into the health and performance of the network.
*   **Faster troubleshooting:** Network observability can help to speed up troubleshooting by providing a more complete picture of what is happening on the network.
*   **Improved performance:** Network observability can help to improve the performance of the network by identifying and resolving problems before they impact users.
*   **Enhanced security:** Network observability can help to enhance security by identifying and responding to threats in real time.

## Conclusion

Network observability is a new and innovative way to monitor complex networks. It offers a number of benefits over the traditional approach, including deeper insights, faster troubleshooting, and improved performance. As networks continue to become more complex, network observability is likely to become an increasingly popular option for monitoring them.
